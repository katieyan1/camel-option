How it works

Red moves: price +5. Blue moves: price −5. Every move is an independent fair coin flip. There are 8 moves across 2 legs.

Shares redeem at the final price. Calls pay max(final price −50, 0). The knockout call pays the same unless price ever touches 65; then it permanently pays zero.

Everyone trades on the same quotes. The bank uses the average payoff over all remaining paths, with a 0.25-coin spread on each side. Trades never move the reference price.

10 units per instrument and 10 units traded per window. No borrowing or short selling. Barriers are checked after moves, including the last one, before settlement.

Payout is not profit: subtract what you paid. Highest final cash wins. Play coins only.
